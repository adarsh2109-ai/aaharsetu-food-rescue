NEEV FOUNDATION SUBMISSION

AaharSetu (आहार सेतु)

Hyperlocal Surplus Food Rescue, Verification & Volunteer Logistics Network

1. Executive Summary (प्रोजेक्ट का उद्देश्य)

Every day across India, tonnes of fresh, hygienic, and untouched food from weddings, hotel buffets, and social functions are discarded late at night due to lack of transport logistics. Simultaneously, thousands in nearby night shelters (Rain Baseras) and low-income settlements sleep hungry. AaharSetu bridges this gap in real-time by connecting food donors, volunteer riders, and verified shelters with 2-way OTP authentication.

2. Problem vs. AaharSetu Solution (समस्या और समाधान)

❌ Ground Reality Challenges
  • Midnight Dumping: Surplus wedding food after 11 PM gets thrown away.
  • No Delivery Fleet: Hosts lack vehicles or time to distribute meals.
  • Zero Accountability: No proof whether donated food reached genuine needy people.
✅ AaharSetu Innovation
  • 60-Second Live Listing: Quick dish chips, plate counters & expiry window.
  • Rider & Volunteer Hub: Local two-wheeler riders get instant pickup tasks.
  • 4-Digit Handover OTP: Delivery completes only after shelter shares OTP.

• Zero Landfill Methane: Prevents organic food waste from rotting in urban landfills.
